Multi-Modal Deepfake Detection In progress

2026 - present · Final year project (team of 2)

Detects lip-sync deepfakes by exploiting the disagreement between what a face appears to say and what the audio actually says - a mismatch that survives compression. Five parallel streams (EfficientNet-B0, Xception and DINOv2 for vision, plus lip-sync and emotion cross-modal streams) emit embeddings rather than scores, which an MLP fuses so weak visual artifacts can still combine with a strong lip-sync mismatch. Trained on FakeAVCeleb v1.2; an earlier visual-only stream reached 0.963 accuracy and 0.994 AUC.
